Lower back pain
  • Do: walk daily, strengthen glutes and core, change position often.
  • Don't: stay in bed for days or lift heavy loads while twisting.
High blood pressure
  • Do: steady moderate cardio, breathe normally during effort.
  • Don't: hold your breath while lifting or start with very intense sessions.
Stress and anxiety
  • Do: walk outdoors, practise slow breathing, keep a regular sleep time.
  • Don't: isolate yourself or overtrain to burn it off.
Poor sleep
  • Do: keep regular bed and wake times, exercise earlier in the day.
  • Don't: drink caffeine after midday or train hard right before bed.

Questions, answered.

Is FitLife really free?

Yes. There are no ads and no subscription.

Where is my data stored?

On your own device. FitLife does not send your workouts, check-ins or PIN anywhere.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load, the phone and desktop versions work without internet.

Is this medical advice?

No. FitLife gives general information only. Talk to a doctor before starting a new routine, especially if you have a health condition, and stop exercising if you feel chest pain, dizziness or sharp pain.

Can my family use the same computer?

Yes. Each person creates their own account with a PIN, and the leaderboard shows everyone on that computer.
